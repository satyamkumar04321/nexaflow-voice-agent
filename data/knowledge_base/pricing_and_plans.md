# NexaFlow Cloud Pricing & Plans

## Starter Plan
- **Price:** $49 per seat/month (billed annually) or $59 monthly.
- **Included:** Up to 25 team members, 5,000 automated workflow runs/month, email & Slack support, standard CRM sync (HubSpot, Zoho).
- **Onboarding:** Self-serve setup in under 15 minutes with guided templates.

## Growth Plan (Most Popular)
- **Price:** $99 per seat/month (billed annually) or $119 monthly.
- **Included:** Up to 250 team members, 50,000 automated runs/month, AI ticket triage, HubSpot/Salesforce/Pipedrive bi-directional sync, custom webhooks, and priority SLA (4-hour response).
- **Discount Policy:** 15% volume discount for annual commitments of 50+ seats; 14-day free proof-of-value trial with sandbox data.

## Enterprise Plan
- **Price:** Custom pricing starting at $2,400/month for up to 100 seats (additional seats from $75/seat).
- **Included:** Unlimited workflows, dedicated VPC/single-tenant deployment option, SAML SSO & SCIM, SOC 2 Type II audit pack, 99.95% uptime SLA, and a dedicated Solutions Engineer.
- **Contract Terms:** Flexible quarterly or annual invoicing, MSA/DPA customization included.
